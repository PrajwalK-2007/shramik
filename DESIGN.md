# Shramik Design System

## Tone & Differentiation
Professional, empowering marketplace connecting daily wage workers with seekers. Bold purple & orange branding. Trustworthy, accessible, worker-centric. Enhanced HD photography and iconic hard-hat logo reinforce labor excellence.

## Color Palette

| Purpose | Light | Dark | Use |
|---------|-------|------|-----|
| Primary | `0.55 0.20 263` | `0.68 0.18 263` | Nav, headers, primary actions |
| Secondary | `0.60 0.15 142` | `0.72 0.14 142` | Growth, secondary buttons |
| Accent | `0.70 0.21 65` | `0.72 0.23 65` | CTAs, highlights |
| Success | `0.65 0.18 142` | `0.72 0.16 142` | Verified status |
| Destructive | `0.55 0.22 25` | `0.65 0.19 22` | Errors |

## Signature Detail
Signature Shramik hard-hat logo (24px, 32px, 64px, scalable SVG) in purple with orange accent. Professional HD photography on home hero and profession cards. Consistent branding on header, login, registration, admin. Location-aware worker cards. Animated homepage. Orange accent CTAs.
